5

Writing: your turn on the podcast

120–180 words · about 15 minutes

Imagine Tom has invited you onto Spend a Day With Me. Write what you'd say about your typical working day. Make sure you mention:

  • how your day usually starts and what it involves;
  • what usually happens when something goes wrong, and what you'll do if a plan of yours works out soon;
  • one thing you would change about your day if you could;
  • a decision or event in your past that changed your working life, and how things might have been different.

Напишите 120–180 слов о своём обычном рабочем дне, как будто вы гостья подкаста. Не пользуйтесь переводчиком — нам важен ваш настоящий уровень.
