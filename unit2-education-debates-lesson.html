15. Prepare and hold the debate

Task 14. Choose a motion. Write 3 arguments for and 3 against. Use at least 8 words from the unit.

  • Motion A: Selective schooling does more harm than good.
  • Motion B: University tuition should be free for everyone.
  • Motion C: Curriculum reform should be decided by teachers, not politicians.
Useful language: I strongly believe that… · One key argument is that… · I see your point, but… · On the other hand, … · Admittedly, … · That may be true; however, …
Model arguments for Motion A

Against selective schooling: it encourages elitism; it perpetuates inequalities; it creates a two-tier system; less well-off children have fewer opportunities to excel.

For selective schooling: pupils are chosen for academic reasons; high-achieving pupils can excel; scholarships and bursaries make it possible for less privileged youngsters to enter.

Task 15. Debate. Argue for or against your motion. Use at least 15 words and phrases from the unit. The teacher takes the opposite side.
